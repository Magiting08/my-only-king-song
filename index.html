Original Worship Song

My Only King

A song of surrender, faith, obedience, and praise to Jesus.
Composition by Christopher Renomeron

Verse 1

You have come into the world as the Light, You are the Bread of Life. You always seek me in every place I go, Now this life belongs to You.

Refrain

And now I lift my hands and praise You, Lord, I will obey, and I will follow You.

Chorus 1

I will seek, I will seek Your Word, The Word that became flesh, it's You, Jesus. I'll believe, I'll believe in You, I can do all things through Christ Who strengthens me.

Verse 2

You sacrifice and offer Your life for me, You broke the chains of sin in me. You promised that You will return again, You're my King, my only King.

Refrain

And now I lift my hands and praise You, Lord, I will obey, and I will follow You.

Chorus 2

I will live, I will live and proclaim That You, O Lord, are my Savior and my God, Jesus. And I will share, I will share Your love, Your love that never ends in me, O Lord, Jesus.

Arrangement

Instrumental Refrain Chorus 1 Chorus 2
“You're my King, my only King.”
